Copenhagen

Our first trip to Scandinavia, primarily staying in Denmark! Taking some mini day trips out of Copenhagen to see some smaller towns around the Denmark area as well as a mini trip to Malmo – Some highlights!

Day 1 – Arrival

A smooth trip over with hardly any issues, we begin our sleep deprived day just managing to stay awake for as long as possible!

Day 2 – Walking Tour and Food

We start our second day freshly rested and a light start with a comedy walking tour and eventually our only booked restaurant of the trip! The walking tour was meant to be politically incorrect, but if anything it was just an accurate assessment of all the different foreigners who joined (Americans being shit a common theme haha)
